## Review record

Record the claim, relevant page/block/form key, actual controlling source, source date, date checked, named student owner,
review trigger and disposition in the approved shared record. Distinguish verified/current, dated snapshot, draft, proposed
and unverified. If a source conflicts with approved policy, route the fact to the appropriate student owner; do not change
a program rule or claim approval merely to match a design. A source-pinned test proves behavior, not organizational authority.
